## Overview

As Senior Technical Writer at AuthLab, I lead the technical knowledge base for **WPManageNinja's entire Fluent plugin suite** — a family of WordPress products trusted by more than **1.47 million customers** worldwide.

## The suite

- **FluentCRM** — email marketing & CRM
- **Fluent Forms** — form builder
- **FluentCart** — eCommerce
- **Fluent Support** — helpdesk & ticketing
- **Fluent Community** — community platform
- **FluentSMTP** — email deliverability
- **Fluent Booking** — scheduling
- **WP Social Ninja** — social proof & feeds

## What the work involves

- **User guides & KB articles** that make complex, feature-rich plugins approachable for non-technical site owners.
- **Release notes** that keep a fast-shipping product line legible to its users.
- **Cross-team collaboration** with product managers, developers, marketers, and support to keep every article accurate and current.
- **SEO & content strategy** — keyword research and on-page optimization so users find answers before they file a ticket.

## The philosophy

Documentation isn't the thing you write after the product ships — it's part of the product. Every article is a chance to reduce support load, speed up adoption, and earn a user's trust. That's the standard I hold this knowledge base to: **clear enough to finish, accurate enough to rely on.**
